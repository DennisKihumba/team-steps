Governance

How Team Steps is run

Every healthy chama runs on clear rules, not goodwill alone. This is our governance framework — modelled on standard Kenyan savings-group practice — covering leadership, contributions, record-keeping and how we resolve disagreements.

Status: draft, pending formal adoption. These rules follow common chama/SACCO conventions and are written so members can review, amend, and formally approve them together — the numbers here (penalties, meeting dates) are starting points, not final until the group votes on them.
Leadership

Three officers, shared control of funds

No single person holds or moves group money alone. As is standard for Kenyan chamas and SACCOs, Team Steps is led by three elected officials, and any withdrawal from group funds requires at least two of their signatures/approvals.

Chairperson

Leads meetings & represents the group

Chairs meetings, casts a deciding vote when needed, and represents Team Steps in dealings with banks, M-Pesa, or outside parties.

Secretary

Keeps records & the membership register

Takes minutes at every meeting, maintains the member list and application records, and communicates decisions to the group.

Treasurer

Manages funds & reconciles contributions

Tracks daily saves and monthly Boosts against M-Pesa records, reports the running balance, and co-signs every withdrawal.

Rules of the group

Our by-laws, plainly written

Membership

Who can join, and how

  • Open to Kenyan residents 18 years or older with a valid national ID.
  • A prospective member must be introduced or nominated by an existing member.
  • New members are admitted by a majority vote of current members.
  • Membership is capped at 50 people; we're working towards at least 40 active contributors.
  • Every member signs and agrees to these rules before their first contribution is accepted.
Contributions

The daily save and the monthly Boost

  • Each member contributes KES 50 daily.
  • Each member contributes an additional KES 1,500 "Boost" once per month.
  • Contributions are currently sent via M-Pesa to a personal number and confirmed manually by the Treasurer; this will move to an automatic Paybill/Till once set up.
  • A member who misses daily saves may catch up within the same month; persistent non-payment is reviewed by the officers.
  • Late-payment penalties (if any) are set by member vote and recorded in the minutes — not applied automatically until agreed.
Record-keeping

Transparency members can check for themselves

  • Every contribution is logged against the member's name and M-Pesa reference code.
  • A running statement of total group savings is shared with all members monthly.
  • Minutes are recorded at every meeting and kept on file for members to review.
  • No withdrawal is made from group funds without at least two officers' sign-off.
Meetings

How often we check in as a team

Officers convene a members' meeting at least once a month to review contributions, discuss group business, and vote on any decisions requiring member approval. Additional meetings can be called by the Chairperson or by request of at least a third of members.

Disputes

Resolving disagreements fairly

Disagreements between members are first raised for open discussion at a meeting. If unresolved, the matter goes to the officers for mediation. Members retain the right to raise unresolved disputes with a neutral third party if needed.

Leaving the group

Exit and dissolution

A member wishing to leave gives notice to the officers; the handling of their accumulated contributions is agreed at the time of exit and recorded in the minutes. Dissolving Team Steps entirely requires a two-thirds vote of members, with all funds accounted for and settled before any distribution.

Amendments

Changing these rules

Any change to these rules requires a two-thirds majority vote of active members at a scheduled meeting, with the change recorded in the minutes before it takes effect.
