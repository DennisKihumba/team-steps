Our story

Why we started

Team Steps began with 5 people who agreed on one simple idea: money set aside daily, even in small amounts, adds up faster than money we mean to save "when we can." Rather than wait for a big lump sum, we chose a rhythm — 50 KES every day — and a once-a-month top-up we call the Boost, to give our savings real momentum.

We're growing on purpose too. We started at 5 and we're opening the group to a maximum of 50 members, with a working target of at least 40 active contributors — enough people for the daily and monthly totals to mean something, small enough that everyone still knows everyone.
